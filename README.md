# Stock Study

A visual study guide to how world events move stock prices, explained with real historical cases.

**Live page:** https://masakasakasama.github.io/stock_study/

## What is here

- `index.html` – Part 1: the principles (one formula, four channels, eight lessons with verified cases and charts). Self-contained, no build step.
- `content/cases.json` – all figures, dates and sources used on the page, structured for redesign (e.g. in Claude Design) and for the weekly log.

## Framework

`Price = EPS × P/E`. News reaches EPS or P/E through four channels: interest rates, the exchange rate, the economy and profit outlook, and risk appetite.
